# Renesas RAJ240045 (045A20) Hardware Procedures for 4SU0016

## Why the Roomba Does Not Need SMBus Telemetry
The Roomba battery bay only has **4 contacts**:
- **2 large outer contacts:** `Pack+` and `Pack-` (`+14.4 V..16.8 V` power)
- **2 small inner contacts:** the **10 kOhm NTC thermistor** (`TH` to `Pack-`)

The SMBus pads (`127` SCL and `128` SDA) on the `4SU0016` PCB are only factory test pads and do **not** connect to the Roomba at all. The Roomba charges and discharges purely via `Pack+`, `Pack-`, and the thermistor.

---

## Option 1 — Direct MOSFET Hardware Bypass on `4SU0016` (Simplest, 1 Wire / Fuse)

Look at the high-side power path on the `4SU0016` board (from Section 6.2 of the `RAJ240045` datasheet):

```
Cell4+ (+15.1V) ───┬────[ S   D ]────[ D   S ]────┬───> Pack+ (Header)
                   │      C-FET        D-FET      │
                  VBAT     │G           │G      VIN12 (Pin 26)
                (Pin 22)   │            │
                         [2k]         [2k]
                           │            │
                         CFOUT        DFOUT
                        (Pin 23)     (Pin 25)
```

### Why `C-FET` and `D-FET` cannot be turned on with a simple pull-up resistor on their gates:
- Both `C-FET` and `D-FET` are **high-side N-channel MOSFETs** whose Sources sit at **`+15.1 V`** (`Cell4+` / `Pack+`).
- To turn on an N-channel MOSFET whose Source is at `+15.1 V`, its Gate must be driven to **`+24 V..+25 V`** (`VBAT + 10 V`), which is normally generated by two internal charge-pump step-up circuits inside the `RAJ240045` (`CFOUT` Pin 23 and `DFOUT` Pin 25).
- There is no `+25 V` rail on the board when the `RAJ240045` charge pumps are off, so pulling the gates to `+15.1 V` (`VBAT`) gives `V_GS = 0 V` (still OFF).

### How to bypass the open `C-FET` + `D-FET` switch on `4SU0016`:
1. Since you already bridged the low-side `D6SC4` fuse (`Pack-` to `B-`), the **only** open switch between `Cell4+ (+15.1 V)` and the `Pack+` output header is the series pair **`C-FET` + `D-FET`**.
2. Solder a **10 A – 15 A fuse** (or resettable PPTC fuse / fusible link wire) directly between:
   - **`Cell4+` (`B+`, `+15.1 V` input side of `C-FET`)**, and
   - **`Pack+` (output header pad / output side of `D-FET`)**.
3. **What still works when `C-FET` + `D-FET` are bypassed with a fuse:**
   - The **`Pack+` and `Pack-` header immediately outputs `15.09 V`**, and the **10 kOhm NTC thermistor** on the `4SU0016` header remains connected as normal, so the Roomba will both run and charge the pack!
   - Even better: the `RAJ240045` (`045A20`) remains powered by the 4 cells (`VIN1..VIN4`) and its **internal 500 Ohm per-cell balancing bleeders** and **SMBus live telemetry (`127`/`128` -> ESP32 + ST7789 240x240 screen)** continue to work!
   - *(Safety note: With `C-FET` and `D-FET` bypassed, the BMS cannot open the circuit if a cell is over-discharged if left off the dock for months, so don't leave the Roomba parked off its charging dock with a dead battery for weeks.)*

## Option 2 — Renesas RL78 `TOOL0` 1-Wire Serial Mode (Implemented in `main/rl78_tool0.c`)

If you want the `RAJ240045` (`045A20`) to turn on its own `C-FET` and `D-FET` charge pumps again without bypassing the MOSFETs, the `RMA` failure block in DataFlash (`0x0F1400..0x0F17FF`) can be erased via the chip's hardware `TOOL0` bootloader pin:

- **ESP32 `GPIO 17`** (`PIN_RL78_TOOL0`) -> **RAJ240045 QFN-32 Pin 7 (`TOOL0 / P40`)**
  - Add a **1 kOhm to 3.3 kOhm pull-up resistor** from `TOOL0` (`GPIO 17`) to the ESP32 `3.3V` rail (or `CREG2` Pin 13 on the QFN-32).
- **ESP32 `GPIO 16`** (`PIN_RL78_RESET`) -> **RAJ240045 QFN-32 Pin 8 (`RESET`)**
- **Common `GND`** between ESP32 and `4SU0016`.

On every ESP32 boot, `rl78_tool0_try_unlock()` in `main/rl78_tool0.c` automatically pulses `RESET` with `TOOL0` low, sends `0x3A` (1-wire UART mode at `115200 8N2`), reads the RL78 Silicon Signature & Security flags, issues `Block Erase (0x22)` on DataFlash block `0x0F1400`, and resets the chip back into normal mode.
